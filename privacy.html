Privacy policy

Pepper is a private assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

Pepper accesses Google data, such as email, calendar, contacts and files, only to carry out the owner's requests. That data is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone except the AI model providers that process the owner's requests.

The owner can remove Pepper's access at any time from their Google Account's security settings.
